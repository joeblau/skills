# Conditional policy routing

Use observed features, declared audiences, selected markets and current console forms. Unknown triggers remain unverified. Read each triggered policy in full and add `EXTRA-*` checks for detailed obligations missing from the baseline. Source IDs resolve in `sources.json`.

| Trigger | Review and resulting work |
|---|---|
| Account creation (including linked web creation) | Account/data deletion initiation, confirmation, processor propagation and retained exceptions. Apple in-app deletion and Play in-app plus outside-app request resource differ. Deactivation is not deletion (A-DELETE, G-DELETE). |
| Social/third-party primary login | Assess Apple §4.8 and its exemptions; identify an equivalent privacy-preserving option (often Sign in with Apple). Own-company login alone does not universally require it. Test every login/revocation path (A-REVIEW). |
| Digital goods/features/subscriptions | Classify what is sold, where consumed and each storefront/region. Use StoreKit/Play Billing unless a current applicable exception/program permits another route. Check enrollment, entitlements, disclosure, reporting and regional versions. Never impose a universal no-external-links rule or treat US/EU rules as globally applicable (A-REVIEW §3.1, G-PAYMENTS). |
| Subscriptions | Price/currency, period, trial conversion, renewal and cancellation disclosures; privacy/terms links; entitlement verification, restore, pending/declined payments, refunds, revocation, grace/hold and account deletion interaction. Research platform-specific subscription policy/technical flows (A-IAP, D-BILLING). |
| Personal data sent to third-party AI | Identify exact data/recipient/routing, clear disclosure and explicit permission before sharing under Apple §5.1.2(i); assess Google User Data disclosure/consent and SDK practice. AI providers' real retention/training practices require evidence; tool-action permission is not data-transfer consent (A-REVIEW, G-USER-DATA). |
| Generative AI as app functionality | Google policy scope/exceptions, prevention of prohibited/deceptive content, in-app reporting/flagging and handling of reports. Separately assess Apple's chatbot/software/UGC rules where applicable. Audit all selectable providers and voice/image inputs (G-AI, G-AI-GUIDE, A-REVIEW §4.7). |
| AI-created/edited listing assets | Per-asset Play self-declaration and applicable labeling; do not confuse this with in-app generation/reporting policy (G-AI-ASSETS). |
| Public/shared user content, chat, communities | Terms/content standards, filtering, user/content reporting, blocking and timely moderation; reviewer access to the flows. Private AI output is not automatically public UGC (G-UGC, A-REVIEW §1.2). |
| Social, dating, anonymous/random chat | Current minor-access restrictions, robust age gating where applicable, published child-safety standards, feedback mechanism, escalation/reporting process and point of contact. Category and actual features both matter (G-CHILD-SAFETY, G-AGE-RESTRICT). |
| Children or mixed-age users | Kids/Families rules, parental gates/consent, allowed SDKs/ads, age-appropriate content and metadata, identifier restrictions and relevant child privacy law. Do not equate a low content rating with child-directed status (G-FAMILIES, A-REVIEW §1.3/5.1.4). |
| Ads/tracking | Accurate ads declaration, SDK initialization and consent timing, age-appropriate ads, Apple ATT where tracking definition is met, identifiers and Data safety/privacy answers. Not all analytics is tracking; ad-free claims require evidence (A-PRIVACY, G-USER-DATA, G-PREPARE). |
| Restricted permissions/services | Android SMS/call logs, background location, broad photos/video/files, package visibility, accessibility service, VPN, alarms, full-screen intents, health, foreground services and restricted APIs: eligibility, minimum scope, declarations/approval and required videos/disclosures. Research each used permission's dedicated policy (G-PERMISSIONS, G-DECLARE). |
| AccessibilityService automation | Review G-A11Y-API; improving UI accessibility is separate from using this sensitive API. Current restrictions include autonomous action planning/execution with specified accessibility-tool exceptions; do not assume generic AI agents qualify. |
| Health/medical | Complete Play Health declaration even when answering no health features. When triggered: data permissions, claims and disclaimers, device/regulatory declarations, research consent/ethics and operator eligibility (G-HEALTH, G-HEALTH-GUIDE, A-INFO, A-REVIEW). |
| Finance/loans/crypto/NFTs | Complete Play Financial declaration even for no features. When triggered: licenses per market, listing disclosures, lending restrictions, data permissions, operator identity and tokenized-asset policy (G-FINANCE, G-FINANCE-FORM, A-REVIEW). |
| News, government, gambling, contests, donations or other regulated services | Discover dedicated rules and mandatory declarations from current policy centers/console, eligibility/rights, geographic/age limits and reviewer evidence. The baseline routing entry is not a substitute for that research. |
| Downloaded code/plugins/emulators/agent execution | App sandbox/API/dynamic-code policy and narrow exceptions, native bridges, downloaded modules, user consent and documented reviewable functionality. Desktop shells/updaters do not transfer into a compliant mobile app (A-REVIEW §2.5/4.7, G-POLICIES). |
| Additional form factors/extensions | Phone/tablet/foldable, TV, Wear/watch, car, XR, widgets/App Clips/extensions: distribution target, quality rules, permissions, age/privacy coverage and specific required assets. Add branch-specific checks rather than pretending phone tests cover all (D-QUALITY, G-ASSETS, A-SCREENSHOTS). |
| Markets and laws | Apple trader status declaration (even without EU distribution; verification/display depends on trader/EU status), encryption/export determination, regional permits, privacy/consumer/child/accessibility/AI rules. Establish jurisdiction and service applicability, then source local official rules (A-DSA, A-EXPORT, EU-EAA). |
| Account type and age | Play identity/contact verification, real-device verification for applicable new personal accounts, production-access testing eligibility and current tester/time threshold. Existing/organization accounts are not automatically subject to the new personal-account testing gate (G-ACCOUNT, G-TESTING). |
| Android identity/package verification | Check current rollout, markets, store participation, certified devices, verified developer and package/signing registration. Do not assume Play publishing enrollment proves every regional registration requirement (D-VERIFY). |

## Dated research findings to refresh

As checked 2026-09-30:

- Apple upload rules distinguish iOS/iPadOS minimum deployment target (iOS 13+ since September 9, 2026) from SDK/toolchain (Xcode 26+ with specified 26-generation SDKs since April 28, 2026). (A-REQUIREMENTS)
- Google Play's phone new-app/update target is API 36 since August 31, 2026; existing-app discoverability and non-phone exceptions differ. Extensions must be confirmed in the target console. (G-TARGET)
- Play Billing Library v7's new-app/update deadline passed August 31, 2026; check v8+ or an approved extension and the live support table. (D-BILLING)
- The current Android memory-page page calls for 16 KB support for apps targeting API 35+ on 64-bit devices and states February 1, 2027 for blocking unsupported updates. Older November 2025 advice is not the current deadline evidence; verify the actual console notice before deciding a present violation. (D-PAGES)
- Applicable new Play personal accounts require at least 12 continuously opted-in closed testers for the preceding 14 days before production-access application. Opt-in alone does not prove useful test coverage or approval. (G-TESTING)
- Android verification protections begin September 30, 2026 for participating stores in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand on certified Android 7+ devices, with further rollout stated for 2027. (D-VERIFY)
- Google permission-policy previews show location/contact changes effective January 27, 2027. Compare intended release date with effective dates rather than reporting previews as current violations. (G-PERMISSIONS, G-DEADLINES)

Maintain a release-specific policy delta log. Record old value, new value, authority, effective scope/date and affected task IDs. If official pages disagree, document the conflict and create a focused verification task until the target-specific rule is established.
