Designing line charts in Figma.
Fast, super fast, super fast.
Use the chart line plugin to create your baseline.
Duplicate your line, smash edit object, whack peat,
click on the last vector point to extend the line,
hold shift and connect the end of your line to the start,
fill the chart with your border color,
switch from solid to linear,
select your first gradient value and set it to 20%,
hide the border, shove it behind your line,
back to, add your axis value,
duplicate and label your axis,
select all, super naughty, but whack come on G,
whack your chart and your dashboard card,
woohoo, there's our line chart Designing Figma.
