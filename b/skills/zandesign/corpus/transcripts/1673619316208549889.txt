Designing a responsive card grid in Figma.
Super fast.
Figma just launched all to a layout wrap
that allows you to design responsibly.
Smash off, bang a frame on the page,
chuck in some sneakers, work tape, add your text,
select all, wax your tape, your image to fill container.
Okay, your card, change the image,
if you get your row, update the images,
select all, wax your tape, take your spacing,
select all your cards, add a minimum width,
get to fill container, add it to your page,
switch to fill container, woo-hoo!
There's our fully responsive grid of cards in Figma.
