Animating hamburger menus in Figma. Superfussed.
Wack L for a line, duplicate twice, nudge up your thickness,
widen the middle line and halve the top line,
select all, workshift L and again, lovely jubbly, add a background,
double up, move and rotate your other lines for a cross,
toggle prototype, click drag and drop,
set smart anime, back it up,
whoo, there's our animated hamburger menu.
