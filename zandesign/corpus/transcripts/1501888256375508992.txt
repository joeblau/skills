designing auto layout carousels in Figma.
Super fast!
Work F, bang a frame on the page,
add an image with unsplash,
workshift out, switch from pack to space between,
fix the width and height,
work T, add your title, add supporting text in a chip,
select all, workshift out,
chuck your text in your card, bang an icon in,
lovely job look, create a bunch of duplicates,
vary your card content, select all, workshift out,
chuck it in your design, pull in the edge of your frame,
toggle prototype, switch no scrolling to horizontal,
woohoo, there's our auto layout carousel in Figma.
