Animating liquid switches in Figma.
So fast, whack F, pull your switch frame,
fully round those corners, give it a background, whack F again,
smash in a rectangular frame, fully round your corners,
lovely jablet, squeeze your rectangle into a circle,
duplicate your switch, pop it out again, create another duplicate,
squeeze your rectangle into a circle, bang it over to the right,
create your final duplicate, pull that circle into a rect,
select all, smash create components out, switch to probes,
connect your switches, be sure to connect your last switch to the first to
complete the loop, first interaction to unclick with smart anime,
the second to after delay, the third with unclick with smart anime again,
and set your last one to after delay, whoo-hoo!
There's our animated liquid switches in Figma.
