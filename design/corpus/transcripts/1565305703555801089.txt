animating a sneaker carousel in Figma.
Soo fast, work F, bang a frame on the page,
grab your sneaker, remove the background with the removed BG,
bang it in your frame, rotate it, work T,
add your text, lovely job lit,
chuck in some more sneakers, work K, scale them down,
reduce the width of your frame, create some duplicates,
rotate it back to zero, scale it down,
nudge it to the left, pull in your next sneaker,
scale it up, rotate, repeat the nudge and rotate for each card,
select all, create component set,
switch to prototype, connect your cards,
switch to on drag, chuck it in your design,
woohoo!
There's our animated sneaker carousel in Figma.
