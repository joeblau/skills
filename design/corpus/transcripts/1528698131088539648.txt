Animating heart buttons in Figma.
Soon first, whack F, bang on a frame,
chuck in your icon, mash out, draw a tiny circle,
tube again, scale up, slide it behind your heart,
tube again, set your active color, scale your heart,
and your circles, tube again,
set the opacity to zero for your circle,
scale your heart down, lovely jubbly,
select all, round those corners, switch to prototype,
connect your buttons, set to bouncy,
connect the last two, switch from bouncy to gentle,
woohoo!
There's our animated heart button in Figma.
