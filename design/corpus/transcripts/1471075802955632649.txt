Animating 3D shapes in Spline.
Spline is a banging 3D design tool and I'm going to show you how to animate shapes so fast.
Visit spline.design, you can create a new file for free.
Select a shape, chuck it on the page, add some revolutions,
lovely job late, set a colour, add a state, and an event.
Wish your event type to hover and your transition to spring,
click and pull the green dot up, click and twist the blue arc,
click play in the top nav, woohoo!
There's our 3D animated shape in Spline.
