Designing 3D buttons in Figma.
Soo fast, work T, add your text,
work shift tape, set your padding,
give it a background, a border,
round those corners, lower your tablet,
duplicate your button, fill in your duplicate,
work F, draw frame for your button,
chuck in your dark button, add your outline button on top,
set your button containers to zero,
duplicate your container, slide your button up,
select all, create a component set,
connect your button, switch to price type,
switch to our hovering, switch to bouncy,
chuck in your design, whoa!
There's our animated 3D button in Figma.
