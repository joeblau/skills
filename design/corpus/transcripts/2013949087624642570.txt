Your UI transitions, every designer should use.
First up is the context transition.
This is great when content changes,
but the structure should stay put.
So tabs switching applying filters
or just general state changes.
Next up is the drill transition,
which helps users navigate between levels of hierarchy
in the information architecture of your product.
So list to item, feed to post, summary to full view.
Probably my favorite transition
is the continuity transition.
You can use it when common elements persist
and morph into the next state.
So card to full screen, collapse player to full player
and grid to product page.
