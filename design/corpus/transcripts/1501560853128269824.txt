Animating play buttons in Figma, super fast.
Work F, bang a frame of the page, round those corners,
give it a background, slide your play icon in,
and your pause icon, hide your pause icon.
Work F, draw a larger frame, round those corners,
whack your button in your frame, whack F again,
draw another frame, give it a gradient background,
slide it into your frame, bang the bracket,
lovely jubbly, nudge it below your frame, create a duplicate,
slide your gradient back up,
hide your play button and show your pause,
select all, create component set,
toggle prototype, connect your buttons,
set to smart animate, bump up your milliseconds,
chuck it in your design, woo hoo!
There's our animated play button in Figma.
