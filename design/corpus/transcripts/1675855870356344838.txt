Setting breakpoint variables in Figma, woo, woo, super fast.
Figma's new min and maximum width feature
makes it super easy to design across mobile tablet and desktop.
Open variables, create a new collection called breakpoints,
add a number variable, call it min width, add your devices,
lovely joblet, check in your breakpoint values,
add another variable, call it max width,
check in your value, select your frame, admin width,
apply min width variable, add your max width,
apply your max width variable, bang in a section,
change variable mode, woo, we can now
design with different breakpoints in Figma.
